# Context and next steps

Notes for the report and for after the sprint.

## Related tasks and games

- **Doublets** (Lewis Carroll, 1879): get from one word to another by changing one letter at a time. A path between two fixed ends, through spelling.
- **Bisociation** (Koestler, *The Act of Creation*, 1964): creativity as the meeting of two unrelated frames of thought.
- **Remote Associates Test** (Mednick, 1962; norms by Bowden and Jung-Beeman, 2003): find the one word that links three distant words.
- **Conceptual combination** (Wisniewski, 1990s): how people read pairs like "zebra clam", through shared properties, relations or a mix.
- **Word association norms**: Kent and Rosanoff (1910); Jenkins and Russell (1960) found that answers became more alike between 1910 and 1952; Small World of Words (De Deyne et al., collected mostly 2011 to 2018).
- **Divergent Association Task** (Olson et al., 2021, data collected before ChatGPT); compared with language models by Bellemare-Pepin et al. (2026).
- **Wikispeedia** (West and Leskovec, 2012): people navigating Wikipedia from one article to another, through a few central hubs.
- **Linxicon** (Trainwreck Labs, 2024): a daily web game where players link two random words through a chain of single words. Bridges leaves the form open (stories, memories, wordplay, chains), has no score, keeps the direction of the arrow, and compares people with AI on the same pairs.

## Future ideas

- **Contact Trainwreck Labs** (trainwrecklabs.com, makers of Linxicon and other free daily educational games). Thousands of players solving the same pair on the same day would give a large human sample of paths and hubs. Also a natural exchange with sofi.games.
- **Ask Jay Olson's group** whether human DAT scores, or how alike people's words are, changed after November 2022.
- **Contested pairs** (freedom → safety, growth → nature), where convergence has a real cost, as a step toward debates on the Map of Ideas.
- **Blind human ratings** of surprise and fit on a mixed sample, with agreement between raters.
- **Creative personas**: processes, people (ordinary lives) and states (playful, dreamy), alongside the investigative FER personas.
- **Show players where their bridges sit** after they finish, as a first small piece of the map.
